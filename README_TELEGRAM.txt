Zona Orsay v0.5.3

IMPORTANT: On first launch, start_server.bat asks for the BotFather token.
Paste the token and press Enter. It is saved locally in telegram_config.py.
Then send /start to the bot in Telegram.

If /start does not respond, check that the console says:
[Telegram] bot enabled

The selected TV LAN address is printed at startup, with 192.168.88.x preferred.
